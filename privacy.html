Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 16, 2026

Isar Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Art Conversation Game mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

The App collects minimal data necessary for its functionality:

  • Session data: temporary session codes and player answers exchanged during gameplay. This data is stored on our servers only for the duration of a game session and is not retained afterwards.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: we use Firebase Crashlytics, a service by Google, to collect anonymous crash reports and diagnostic information (such as device model, operating system version, and crash stack traces). This data is used solely to identify and fix technical issues.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Location data
  • Photos or media from your device
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — for crash reporting and diagnostics. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — for hosting game session infrastructure. Session data is processed on AWS servers and not shared with third parties.

Data Retention

Game session data (player answers, session codes) is temporary and deleted when a session ends. Crash diagnostic data is retained by Firebase Crashlytics according to Google's data retention policies.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
